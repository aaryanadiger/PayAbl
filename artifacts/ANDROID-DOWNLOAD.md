# Payabl for Android

[Download Payabl.apk](https://expo.dev/artifacts/eas/WdejSQphelXOWVTcvcG45GiaZFPNljFTcAk_i9pV8xY.apk)

Open the link on an Android phone, download the APK, and open the file to install. Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser. The installed app does not require Expo Go or a running development server.

Pay family requests contact access, then microphone access for voice entry. Spoken guidance is enabled automatically. Account and Settings are available through the person and wheel icons. Payments remain simulated.

An APK installs on Android only. iPhone testing continues through Expo Go; Siri integration and native speech recognition are not available inside Expo Go.
